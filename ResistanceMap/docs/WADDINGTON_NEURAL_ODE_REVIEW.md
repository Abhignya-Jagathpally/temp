## 1. Setup and notation

Let $X \in \mathbb{R}^{D}$ denote a high-dimensional molecular state (RNA, proteomic, or fused latent of dimension $D$). A **landscape** is a scalar function $V_\theta : \mathbb{R}^D \to \mathbb{R}$ together with a stochastic dynamical system

$$
dX_t \;=\; -\nabla V_\theta(X_t)\,dt \;+\; \sqrt{2D_0}\,dW_t \quad\quad (1)
$$

where $D_0$ is a (possibly state-dependent) diffusion. The Boltzmann steady state $P_{ss}(x)\propto e^{-V(x)/D_0}$ exists when $\nabla\!\times\! f = 0$ (gradient field). For non-gradient drift, Wang et al. decompose

$$
f(x) \;=\; -\nabla U(x) + j_{ss}(x)/P_{ss}(x), \quad \nabla\!\cdot\!j_{ss}=0 \quad\quad (2)
$$

so a **quasi-potential** $U=-\log P_{ss}$ always exists, but is generated by a non-zero curl current $j_{ss}$ (Wang, Zhang, Xu, Wang 2011, PMID 21536909).

**Problem statement.** Given a single snapshot $x_0$ of one MM patient at the start of second-line therapy, predict the *trajectory* $\{x_t\}_{t\in[0,T]}$ over $T=12$ months and a derived clinical scalar (PFS, response category). Train using:

* $N_b = 994$ baseline-only snapshots (treat as $P_{ss}$ samples),
* $N_p = 42$ paired multi-visit patients (only longitudinal signal),
* $N_r = 319$ Recurrent BM cross-sectional (treat as second mode of $P_{ss}$, post-relapse),
* PPI $G=(V_g,E_g)$, $|V_g|=20{,}000$, $|E_g|=473{,}860$ at confidence $\ge 0.7$, with graph Laplacian $L_{PPI}=D-A$.

**What is identifiable from a single snapshot?** Without longitudinal data, only the *empirical density* $\hat P(x)$ is identifiable, hence $\hat U(x) = -\log \hat P(x) + c$ up to additive constant. The drift decomposition (2) is **not** identifiable without time-resolved data: any divergence-free $j_{ss}$ is consistent with the same density. This is the *Helmholtz gauge non-identifiability*. Paired data ($N_p=42$) breaks the gauge, but only along directions sampled in the 42 patient-pair displacements — a 42-dimensional subspace of the $D$-dimensional velocity space.

## 3. Three unique angles — formal derivations

### 3.1 Energy-based potential learning ONLY

Rather than learning a vector field $f_\theta : \mathbb{R}^D\to\mathbb{R}^D$ ($O(D^2)$ parameters in a 2-layer net), learn a scalar $V_\theta : \mathbb{R}^D\to\mathbb{R}$ ($O(D)$ params). The drift is $f_\theta(x) = -\nabla_x V_\theta(x)$, automatically curl-free.

**Training via denoising score matching (DSM):** add Gaussian noise $\sigma$ to clean samples, $\tilde x = x + \sigma\eta$, $\eta\sim\mathcal{N}(0,I)$. The Vincent (2011) identity gives

$$
\mathcal{L}_{DSM}(\theta) \;=\; \mathbb{E}_{x\sim p_{data},\,\eta}\Big[\,\big\|-\nabla_x V_\theta(\tilde x)\;-\;(x-\tilde x)/\sigma^2\big\|^2\Big] \quad\quad (3)
$$

Minimizing (3) yields $\nabla V_\theta(x) \to -\nabla\log p_{data}(x)$ in the limit $\sigma\to 0$, hence $V_\theta\to -\log p_{data}$ up to additive constant — exactly Wang's quasi-potential when the drift is gradient (Song & Ermon 2019, arXiv 1907.05600, HTTP 200).

**Identifiability:** $V$ is identifiable up to an additive constant iff the data support is path-connected (else only piecewise on each connected component). For MM where $N_b=994$ samples likely lie on a connected manifold of cytogenetic strata, identifiability holds globally, but **only on the support** — points far from any training sample have $V_\theta$ extrapolated arbitrarily (a critical caveat for $t=12$ month forecasts that may drift off-manifold).

**Sample complexity:** Hyvärinen-style score matching achieves $\|s_\theta - \nabla\log p\|_{L^2}^2 = O(N^{-1/2})$ under standard regularity (bounded Hessian of $\log p$, Lipschitz $s_\theta$). For $N_b=994$ this is RMSE $\approx 0.18$ in score units — adequate for basin identification but insufficient for fine-grained drift fields.

**Why this matters:** $V$ has $O(D)$ params vs $O(D^2)$ for $f_\theta$, ~50× fewer trainable parameters at $D=64$. Basins $=$ local minima of $V$ are interpretable as "epigenetic memory states." Pontryagin attribution: $\partial V/\partial x_i$ at $x_0$ ranks driver coordinates for the resistance trajectory. **This is the strongest candidate for ResistanceMap.**

### 3.2 PPI-Laplacian regularized velocity field

The PPI graph defines a Laplacian $L_{PPI} = D - A$ on the $D$-dim feature space ($D \approx |V_g|$ if features are gene-level; $D=64$ if features are a learned latent — in which case PPI must be projected to the latent via the encoder Jacobian).

**Inductive bias.** Regularize the velocity to be smooth on the PPI graph:

$$
\mathcal{L}_{PPI}(\theta) \;=\; \lambda_1 \,\langle f_\theta,\, L_{PPI}\, f_\theta\rangle \;+\; \lambda_2\,\|\nabla\!\cdot\! f_\theta - \alpha\, L_{PPI}\,x\|^2 \quad\quad (4)
$$

The first term is the Belkin-Niyogi 2003 Laplacian eigenmaps prior (DOI 10.1162/089976603321780317; publisher anti-bot 403 but DOI canonical) — it forces $f$ to lie in the span of the low-frequency Laplacian eigenmodes $\{\phi_k\}_{k\le K}$. The effective velocity dimension drops from $D$ to $K$ (typically $K\!=\!50$–$200$ for PPI), a **massive variance reduction** but at the cost of expressivity.

**Justification:** if biological dynamics propagate predominantly along PPI edges (physical interactions or co-regulation), then $f$ has Fourier energy concentrated in low $L_{PPI}$ modes. This is the assumption underlying network-propagation methods (Cowen et al. 2017 review, citation deferred — not load-bearing).

**Risk:** if drug resistance is driven by *off-PPI* mechanisms (epigenetic, post-translational), this prior is misspecified. Falsifiable — see §5.

### 3.3 Hamiltonian / symplectic structure for MM resistance trajectories?

Greydanus et al. 2019 (arXiv 1906.01563, HTTP 200) parameterize $f$ as $f = J\nabla H$, $J$ symplectic, conserving $H$ exactly. **This is the wrong inductive bias for MM:** drug-induced resistance is *strongly dissipative* — clones go extinct, the population concentrates onto fewer attractors, entropy decreases inside basins. A symplectic system would conserve phase-space volume and forbid attractors, which contradicts the entire premise of "epigenetic memory states."

**Conclusion:** reject Hamiltonian prior. The correct prior is overdamped Langevin (eq. 1) — a *gradient-flow plus noise* system, exactly what energy-based potential learning (§3.1) provides.

## 4. Decision-table verdict for our regime

Regime: $N_b=994$ baseline + $N_p=42$ paired + $N_r=319$ Recurrent BM + STRING PPI.

| Criterion | Energy-based $V_\theta$ (§3.1) | TrajectoryNet | CellOT | JKO | Wang quasi-pot |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Snapshot-only viable | yes | weak | needs paired pops | needs $\ge 2$ times | yes |
| Sample efficiency at $N=994$ | strong ($\sqrt N$) | weak ($N_p=42$ for $f$) | medium (319 vs 994) | weak | strong |
| PPI integration tractability | clean (Tikhonov on $V$) | clean (GNN $f$) | clean (ICNN) | medium | clean |
| Interpretability of basins | direct (minima of $V$) | indirect | indirect | indirect | direct |
| Compute cost | low (1 scalar net) | high (CNF) | medium | high (iterated JKO) | low |
| Identifiability under regime | up to const, on support | curl part fragile | Brenier map ok | $V$ identifiable | gradient ok |

**Recommendation:**

* **Primary: Energy-based potential $V_\theta$ trained by denoising score matching (§3.1) with PPI-Laplacian Tikhonov regularizer (§3.2)**, using all $N_b+N_r=1313$ snapshots as samples of a (mixture) steady-state. The 42 paired patients enter as *anchored bridges* — auxiliary loss $\lambda_3 \|x_{p,t_1} - \mathrm{Flow}_{V_\theta}(x_{p,t_0}, \Delta t)\|^2$ — to break the curl gauge along observed displacement directions.
* **Fallback: CellOT (Bunne 2023, PMID 37903912)** treating Recurrent BM (319) as the perturbed population vs baseline (994) as control, learning a Brenier map $T = \nabla\psi$ from baseline to relapse with PPI-Laplacian regularization on $\psi$. This sidesteps the need for *temporal* trajectories and directly answers "what does this baseline patient look like at relapse." Loses the time axis; gains identifiability and sample efficiency.

Both compatible with the v10 spec's hierarchical-Bayes outer layer (`docs/V10_PER_PATIENT_FORECAST_SPEC.md`); replace the inner Trajectory Flow Matching with energy-based $V_\theta$ as the primary inner-loop forward model.

## 5. Falsification test

**Claim under test:** Energy-based $V_\theta$ + PPI Tikhonov gives well-calibrated 12-month forecasts.

**Experiment.** Hold out the $N_p=42$ paired patients. Train $V_\theta$ on the remaining $994-N_p^{(t_0)}+319=1271$ snapshots. For each held-out paired patient $i$ with $(x_{i,0}, x_{i,1}, \Delta t_i)$:

1. Simulate forward $\hat x_{i,1} = \mathrm{Flow}_{-\nabla V_\theta}(x_{i,0}, \Delta t_i)$ for $\Delta t_i\in[3,12]$ months.
2. Compute Euclidean error $e_i = \|\hat x_{i,1} - x_{i,1}\|_2$ in the latent space.
3. Compute baseline error $e_i^{(0)} = \|x_{i,0} - x_{i,1}\|_2$ (no-motion predictor).
4. Compute $r_i = e_i / e_i^{(0)}$.

**Falsification threshold.** If the median $r_i \ge 0.9$ across the 42 patients (i.e., $V_\theta$ does not improve over the trivial "no-change" baseline by more than 10%), the energy-based formulation is rejected for our regime. This is a strict test because the no-motion predictor is already strong at short horizons.

**Secondary threshold (identifiability check).** Train two independent $V_\theta$ instances with different random seeds. Compute $\|V_\theta^{(1)}(x) - V_\theta^{(2)}(x) - c\|$ on a held-out set with the optimal constant $c$. If this exceeds 1 nat per latent dim, identifiability is failing in practice and the recommendation downgrades to fallback.

**Dataset spec for the test.** $N_p=42$ paired (RNA at $\ge 2$ visits + 2nd-line transition) is the only longitudinal probe. Reserve all 42 for evaluation; *do not* include any of them in training. Monte Carlo over 5 random splits of the 994+319 unpaired snapshots only.

## 6. Paths NOT chosen — explicit rejections

* **Hamilton-Jacobi only (Lv 2014).** Computing the action $S[\phi]$ requires solving a $D$-dim BVP per query point. With $D=64$, the curse of dimensionality makes this intractable; any $D=2$ projection sacrifices the multi-omic coupling that motivated this pipeline.
* **Schrödinger Bridge without prior (Schiebinger 2019, PMID 30712874).** The bridge between baseline and Recurrent BM cohorts is a *population* coupling, not a per-patient coupling. With $N_p=42$ paired patients, we can identify the bridge in only a 42-dim subspace; the remaining $D-42$ dims are gauge-free. Bridge alone $\Rightarrow$ underdetermined; needs informative prior.
* **Hamiltonian Neural Networks (Greydanus 2019).** Conservative dynamics contradict dissipative resistance evolution (§3.3).
* **Pure CNF / TrajectoryNet (Tong 2020) without PPI.** Without inductive bias, CNF needs $\gg 42$ paired samples to constrain the velocity field; PPI as graph prior is essential for our $N_p$.
* **scNODE / dynamo (vector-field on velocity).** Require spliced/unspliced RNA velocity, which our MMRF data do not provide (bulk RNA-seq, not 10x).
* **Wang quasi-potential as primary.** Same gradient identifiability as energy-based, but the original 2011 formulation does not specify a tractable parametric inference scheme for high-dim $D$. Score matching (§3.1) is the modern operationalization of the same idea.

## 8. Connection to existing ResistanceMap code

The current `resistancemap/models/trajectory.py` (`ChromatinODE`, `TrajectoryForecaster`) implements a **2-state Sneppen-Ringrose chromatin ODE** that is *not* a Waddington landscape in the sense above — it is a fixed bistable ODE with $a,r\in\mathbb{R}^2$ and 8 protein-derived parameters, integrated by Euler. The full $D=64$ VAE latent enters only as a $\pm 20\%$ multiplicative modulation of those 8 params (`_latent_to_ode_params` line 884). Consequently:

* The existing model has **2 dynamical degrees of freedom**, not 64. Calling its output a "cell-state trajectory" overstates resolution.
* The basin-depth scorer (`_estimate_basin_depth`) computes a 2x2 Jacobian at the steady state — this is well-defined but is *not* a learned landscape; it is a closed-form linearization of a hand-crafted ODE.
* Migrating to energy-based $V_\theta$ on the full 64-d latent (plus PPI Tikhonov) would replace `ChromatinODE` entirely with a single MLP $V_\theta:\mathbb{R}^{64}\to\mathbb{R}$ and integrate $\dot z = -\nabla V_\theta(z)$ via `torchdiffeq`. The existing `TrajectoryForecaster` scaffolding (horizon scaling, reliability scorer) can be kept.

This audit recommends that change as the v10 inner-loop forward model.
